# Retail Sales Performance Dashboard (2025)

## Dashboard
**Workbook:** `Retail_Sales_Performance_Dashboard.twbx`  
**Dashboard title:** Retail Sales Performance Dashboard | 2025

### Dashboard KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value

### Views included in the workbook
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Quantity by Category
- Sales by Segment
- Product Sales Treemap
- Sales by State
- Discount vs Profit
- Orders by Shipping Mode


## Analytical questions explored
- How do sales change over the months?
- Which categories and sub-categories contribute the most sales?
- How do sales and profit differ across categories and regions?
- How is sales distributed across customer segments and shipping modes?
- How do discount levels relate to profit in the simulated records?
- How do sales vary across the states represented in the dataset?
