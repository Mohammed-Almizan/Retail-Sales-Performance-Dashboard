### Overall business takeaways
-	Revenue concentration: Electronics and Furniture account for approximately 69.3% of total sales.
-	Regional performance: South is the leading region by sales and absolute profit.
-	Customer mix: Consumer customers contribute approximately 64.5% of revenue.
-	Profitability: Clothing has the highest category profit margin, even though Electronics has the highest absolute profit.

### Recommendations
-	Inventory: monitor stock availability for Electronics and Furniture, the largest sales categories.
-	Regional strategy: investigate drivers of South's stronger performance and barriers in North.
-	Customer strategy: assess the potential to grow Corporate sales while preserving its comparatively higher margin.
